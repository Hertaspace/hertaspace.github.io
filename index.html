  1. Nov 7Proved

    All Weyl–Heisenberg SICs and ghosts in dimensions 4 to 7

    Computer-aided proofs that the complexified Weyl–Heisenberg SIC equations have exactly 512, 4000, 6912 and 32928 solutions in dimensions 4 to 7. Half are SIC fiducials and, in these dimensions, the other half are ghosts. In dimensions 6 and 7 every solution has a Zauner symmetry of order 3, so the strong Zauner conjecture holds there.   2. Oct 25Formalized

    SIC-POVMs in every dimension, formalized in Lean 4

    A machine-checked Lean 4 proof that SIC-POVMs exist in every finite dimension, the existence part of Zauner's conjecture. It follows the proof of Radchenko and Wheeler, with a theorem of Appleby, Flammia and Kopp. Written by AI agents under human direction. Accepted by six kernels. Repository
