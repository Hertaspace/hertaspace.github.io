SIC-POVMs in every dimension, formalized in Lean 4

Yaoling Yang · 25 October 2026

A machine-checked Lean 4 proof that SIC-POVMs exist in every finite dimension, the existence part of Zauner's conjecture. It follows the proof of Radchenko and Wheeler, with a theorem of Appleby, Flammia and Kopp. Written by AI agents under human direction. Accepted by six kernels.

Statement

For every integer \(d \ge 1\) there exist \(d^2\) unit vectors \(\psi_i\) in \(\mathbb{C}^d\) with

$$ |\langle \psi_i, \psi_j \rangle|^2 = \frac{1}{d+1} \qquad \text{for all } i \neq j. $$

Such a family is the standard description of a symmetric informationally complete POVM (SIC-POVM). The statement is copied from Google DeepMind's formal-conjectures, where it is the existence formulation of Open Quantum Problem 23. The project also proves an analogue for rank-\(r\) SICs, whenever \(r \ge 1\), \(d > 2r + 1\) and \(r(d-r)\) divides \(d^2 - 1\).

Credit

The mathematics is due to D. Radchenko and C. Wheeler (arXiv:2609.21892) and to M. Appleby, S. T. Flammia and G. S. Kopp (arXiv:2501.03970). Māris Ozols has an earlier, independent Lean 4 formalization of the same existence results, marozols/SIC-existence. The Lean code here was written by large language model agents under human direction.

Checks

The proof uses only the axioms propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound, and six kernels accept it. The project has 443 files and about 110,000 lines of Lean, and it builds on Mathlib. The repository has the full proof outline, sources and verification records.
